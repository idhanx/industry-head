The hidden factory

Your losses are real money. Today they live in registers, WhatsApp and memory.

A breakdown, a changeover that ran long, a batch on hold, a line two people short — each costs money, but nobody sees the total until month-end. Industryhead prices each loss as it happens, ranks it, and makes sure someone owns the fix.

  • Every rupee traceable to the stop or inspection behind it.
  • Each loss counted once — never double-charged across reports.
  • Built from your own rates, routings and records.

Getting started

Four steps from first meeting to first verified saving.

Map the plant

We collect your machines, parts, routings, rates and shifts in ready-made templates and load your history.

Go live on tablets

Shift start, counts, stops and inspections on the floor — no wiring and no new hardware to begin.

See the rupees

Industryhead prices every loss. The morning meeting starts from facts, not opinions.

Fix and verify

Actions get owners and dates. Savings are measured against a frozen baseline before they count.

Your data stays yoursEach plant's records are isolated at the database level, with role-based access from owner to operator.

Numbers you can auditEvery figure traces back to its source entry, and an activity log shows who changed what.

Works when the Wi-Fi doesn'tFloor entries are kept offline and sync when the connection returns — nothing lost mid-shift.
